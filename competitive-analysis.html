Section 03

Our Position

The uncontested position: No product in market combines high protein (18g), clean caffeine (80mg), targeted micronutrient density (9 FDA-claimable nutrients), zero added sugar, and a 12-ingredient whole-food label. David wins on protein math. RXBAR wins on ingredient simplicity. Neither addresses micronutrient deficiency. Our bar is the only product purpose-built for the clinical nutrition needs of GLP-1 patients — a market growing to 30 million Americans by 2030.
