Section 01

The GLP-1 Nutrition Gap

Over 12 million Americans are currently prescribed GLP-1 medications (semaglutide, tirzepatide) for weight management. These drugs suppress appetite, reducing daily intake to 1,200–1,500 calories — creating a critical nutrition paradox: patients eat dramatically less while their bodies still require the same essential vitamins and minerals. Published research from ACLM, ASN, OMA, and TOS documents alarming deficiency rates across GLP-1 populations.

Deficiency % of GLP-1 Users Affected This Bar Delivers Key Whole-Food Sources in Bar
Magnesium 89.9% 33% DV (140mg) Pumpkin seeds, cacao, almond butter, hemp
Iron 88.4% 16% DV (2.9mg) Cacao, pumpkin seeds, tahini, hemp seeds
Protein (sarcopenia) Risk: 8% → 32% 36% DV (18g) Egg white, almond butter, pumpkin seeds, collagen
Zinc Commonly deficient 18% DV (2.0mg) Pumpkin seeds, hemp seeds, cacao
Potassium 98.6% 8% DV (370mg) Dates, cacao, almond butter, pumpkin seeds
Choline 94.2% ~5% DV (~27mg) Egg whites, seeds
Vitamin D 98.6% 0% DV Not addressable via whole foods in bar format

Honest assessment: This bar meaningfully addresses 5 of 7 major GLP-1 deficiencies through whole-food sources alone. Vitamin D and potassium cannot be adequately concentrated in a 60g bar without synthetic fortification. The D2C subscription model creates an opportunity to bundle vitamin D drops or electrolyte packets as add-ons — addressing these gaps without compromising the bar's clean-label integrity.

Market validation: Nestlé launched Vital Pursuit (frozen meals for GLP-1 users) in 2024 to national retail distribution at Walmart, Target, and Kroger — the first major CPG brand to explicitly target this population. No clean-label snack bar has entered this space with a micronutrient-first formulation. J.P. Morgan predicts total GLP-1 users will reach 30 million by 2030.
